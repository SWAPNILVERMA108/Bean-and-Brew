Our story

A simpler way to explore coffee.

Bean & Brew is a Django-powered coffee guide that makes it easy to explore varieties, learn what makes each cup special, and locate stores that offer them.

The project brings together clear information, simple search, and a warm, focused experience—so the technology stays in the background and the coffee stays at the centre.
